The HUNYKHO Story

2018 - The Spark

HUNYKHO begins experimenting with electronic music production in a small home studio, finding a unique voice by blending synth-pop with sharp social commentary.

2020 - Digital Dystopia

Release of the debut EP, "Digital Dystopia". The record gains critical acclaim for its bold critique of modern society and its innovative sound.

2021 - Golden Threads

A shift in focus with the single "Golden Threads", a heartfelt ballad that explores human connection, showing a new, warmer side of the artist's work.

2022 - Neon Rebellion Tour

First international tour. The live shows are praised for their immersive visuals and the powerful dual-themed setlist, solidifying HUNYKHO's status as a visionary performer.

2023 - Synthetic Hearts

The first full-length album, "Synthetic Hearts", is released, perfectly balancing the cold, futuristic critique with warm, emotional storytelling. It tops alternative charts worldwide.
